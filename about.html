About Me

Hi! My name is Aarush Prakash and I am a student in the Technology Management for IT program at Ontario Tech University. Ever since I first found out about computers, I have always been fascinated by the way the machine works. This curiosity is what made me pursue IT as a career path.

Growing up, I also found a keen interest in modding gaming consoles, whether it was a PSP, an Xbox 360 or a PS3, and running homebrew applications on them. This helped me learn how different systems have different architectures for running programs and services.

I have also built my own home lab with a four-node Proxmox cluster, where I can simulate a real production environment and use it as a hands-on way to keep learning.

My goal is to become a DevOps engineer. I like the idea of connecting the people who build software with the people who run it, and making the process of testing and releasing applications faster and more reliable.

Through my program I am learning both the technical side of IT and how technology is managed in an organization. This website is one of my first web projects, built with HTML5 and CSS3.
